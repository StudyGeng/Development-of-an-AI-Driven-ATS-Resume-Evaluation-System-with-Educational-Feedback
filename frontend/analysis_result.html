    Privacy Policy

    Data use for UTS CS Career Navigator

    We collect only the information needed to run resume analysis for UTS Computer Science students.

    Uploaded resumes are processed to generate ATS feedback, keyword matching, and improvement suggestions.

    User account data stores name, email, and hashed password for sign-in and saved analysis history.

    Do not upload sensitive third-party information without permission. For production use, add HTTPS and retention controls.

    Terms of Service

    Guidelines for using the UTS CS career tool

    This tool provides resume feedback for UTS Computer Science students and is intended as guidance only.

    Users are responsible for the accuracy of uploaded resumes and selected target roles.

    Do not upload confidential or third-party material unless you have permission to do so.

    Research Credits

    Methods used in the project

    The app uses PDF extraction, TF-IDF similarity scoring, and simple rule-based feedback generation.

    The layout and workflows were shaped to support resume preparation for UTS Computer Science students.
