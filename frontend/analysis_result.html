    Privacy Policy

    Data use for UTS CS Career Navigator

    We use account details and resume content to provide this educational tool.

    Uploaded resumes are processed to generate ATS feedback, keyword matching, and improvement suggestions.

    Your account stores your name, email, and a password hash. Saved analysis records can include text extracted from your resume.

    Do not upload confidential or third-party information without permission. Review the feedback before using it in a job application.

    Terms of Service

    Guidelines for using the UTS CS career tool

    This tool provides resume feedback for UTS Computer Science students and is intended as guidance only.

    Users are responsible for the accuracy of uploaded resumes and selected target roles.

    Do not upload confidential or third-party material unless you have permission to do so.

    Research Credits

    Methods used in the project

    The app uses PDF text extraction, optional image OCR, TF-IDF similarity scoring, and rule-based feedback generation.

    The layout and workflows were shaped to support resume preparation for UTS Computer Science students.

    About This Project

    Built for student resume learning

    This student project was developed for University of Technology Sarawak (UTS), Malaysia. It provides educational resume feedback and does not represent an official university recruitment service.

    Its role comparisons and ATS checks are guidance for revision, not a guarantee of interviews or employment.
